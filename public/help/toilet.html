Toilet overflowing or running? Do this first

An overflowing toilet is scary, but you can stop the water in a few seconds. Most running or weak-flush problems are a flapper, a chain, or a float you can see under the tank lid.

Smell gas, or is a carbon monoxide alarm sounding? Skip this page. Leave the house, then call 911 or your gas utility's emergency line from outside.
  1. Stop the water

    Turn the shutoff valve on the wall behind the toilet clockwise until it stops. No valve, or it will not turn? Lift the tank lid and pull the float up to stop the fill.

  2. Do not flush again

    A second flush on a clogged toilet sends more water over the rim. Wait until the bowl drains down on its own.

  3. Try a plunger the right way

    Use a flange plunger (the kind with a sleeve on the bottom). Seal it over the hole and push and pull firmly about ten times, keeping it under water.

  4. Look in the tank for a running toilet

    If it runs nonstop, check whether the chain is tangled or the rubber flapper is not sealing. Straighten the chain so it has a little slack.

  5. Check the water level

    Water spilling into the tall tube in the middle of the tank means the float is set too high. Bending or adjusting the float down usually stops it.

  6. Check other drains

    If only this toilet is slow, the clog is in the toilet. If tubs and sinks are slow too, the problem is further down the line.

Stop and call a pro if

  • Water or sewage comes up in a tub, shower, or floor drain when you flush or run water.
  • The shutoff valve leaks or breaks when you turn it.
  • Water is leaking from the base of the toilet onto the floor.
  • Plunging does not clear it after a few tries.

While you wait

  • Leave the supply valve off until the problem is fixed.
  • Use another bathroom, and if several drains are backing up, stop running water in the house.
  • Wipe up overflow water quickly and keep kids and pets away from it.

These are general checks, not a diagnosis. If anything seems unsafe, stop and call a licensed professional.
