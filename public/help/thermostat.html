Thermostat not working? Check these first

A blank or unresponsive thermostat is often a power problem somewhere else in the system. These checks take a few minutes.

Smell gas, or is a carbon monoxide alarm sounding? Skip this page. Leave the house, then call 911 or your gas utility's emergency line from outside.
  1. Replace the batteries

    Many thermostats run on AA or AAA batteries behind the faceplate. Swap them even if the screen still shows faintly.

  2. Check the breaker and furnace switch

    Most thermostats get power from the furnace or air handler. A tripped breaker or a switched-off unit leaves the screen blank.

  3. Close the furnace door

    A safety switch cuts power when the furnace panel is loose. Make sure the door is fully seated.

  4. Check the mode and schedule

    Make sure it is set to Heat or Cool, not Off, and that a schedule or vacation hold is not overriding you.

  5. Check for water in summer

    A full AC drain pan can trip a safety switch that also cuts power to the thermostat.

  6. Restart a smart thermostat

    Use its restart option in the menu, or pull it off the wall plate for a minute and put it back.

Stop and call a pro if

  • The screen stays blank after new batteries and a breaker check.
  • The thermostat clicks but nothing starts.
  • The breaker trips again after you reset it.
  • The system runs constantly and ignores the setting.

While you wait

  • Write down the thermostat brand and model for the contractor.
  • If the system is stuck on, you can turn it off at its breaker.
  • In freezing weather, protect pipes by opening cabinet doors and letting faucets drip.

These are general checks, not a diagnosis. If anything seems unsafe, stop and call a licensed professional.
