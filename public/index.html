Questions

What does it cost me?

Nothing to use . You pay the contractor directly for the visit and any repair you approve.

Who are the contractors?

Independent New Jersey HVAC companies. Each one gives us its state HVACR license number, and we check it before they can receive requests. They aren't our employees, and they set their own prices.

How fast will someone answer?

It depends on who's free near you. Your request page shows each yes as it comes in, and after 15 minutes with no answer we show contractors you can call directly.

What if no one can come?

We tell you and show contractors you can call directly. Your request stays open for 12 hours, and we email you if someone accepts later.

Who sees my phone number?

Only the one contractor you choose. Contractors who respond see your ZIP code and the problem, not your name, number, or address.

Do you do plumbing or electrical?

Not yet. We're starting with heating and cooling so we can do it well.
