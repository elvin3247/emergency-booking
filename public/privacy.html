What we collect

  • From homeowners: the problem, ZIP code, county, timing, any note you write, your name, phone, email, and street address.
  • From pros: company name, contact name, email, mobile and business phone, license number, trades, counties, visit fee, and alert hours.
  • Reviews: the rating and any comment you leave after a visit.
  • Automatically: which page or partner link sent you here, and a scrambled version of your internet address that we use only to limit spam. We keep page-view counts through Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies.
  • On your device: your browser stores the link to your open request and any half-finished form, so you can pick up where you left off. This stays on your device.

How we use it

  • To alert pros who cover your area. Before you choose, they see only the problem, your ZIP code and county, the timing, and your note.
  • When you choose a pro, we share your name, phone, email, and address with that one company so they can come.
  • To email you about your request, and to text you once we offer text messages.
  • To check licenses, handle complaints, bill lead fees, and keep the service working and safe.

Who we share it with

  • The one pro you choose, as described above.
  • Companies that run the site for us: Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database), Resend (email), and Twilio (text messages).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information. Mobile numbers and text message opt-in consent are never shared with or sold to anyone for their marketing. They are used only to send the messages you agreed to.

How long we keep it

We keep request and account records for [period] for billing, disputes, and legal needs, then delete them or remove the personal details.

Your choices

  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, at [contact email].
  • Pros can pause alerts or change their details on their settings page.
  • You can reply STOP to any text message.
